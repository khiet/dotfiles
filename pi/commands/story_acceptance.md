# Story Acceptance

Use the `story-acceptance` skill to turn the finished story into an acceptance document a Playwright agent can execute and report back on.

Usage: `/story_acceptance [issue id] [extra instructions]`

Pass the extra instructions to the skill as requirements, such as a mock design to test against, the environment to use, or areas to leave out.
